Shaderbooth

Your camera, through up to six stacked GLSL effects, on the GPU. Nothing leaves this page — the video never goes to a server, and the recordings are written by your own browser.

SpaceStart / stop the camera
TabNext camera
Ctrl + ← / →Previous / next camera
[ / ]Previous / next effect, in the active layer
− / +Effect intensity, in the active layer
0Reset intensity to the effect's default
1 – 6Edit that layer of the stack
A / DAdd a layer above the active one / delete it
, / .Move the active layer down / up the stack
MMirror horizontally
RStart / stop recording the output
SSave a still of the output
FFullscreen
EscClose this, or leave fullscreen

Stacking is the point: Halftone over Edge Glow prints the edges as dots, Edge Glow over Halftone finds the outline of the dots. The numbered tabs are the stack, bottom layer first.
